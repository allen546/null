Null control hierarchy

Control runs from one commander (an LLM, or a person, with a person above it for approvals) down to raw Azalea bot operations on a vanilla server. Each level talks only to its parent and its children. Intent goes down, and down never carries per-bot actions. Fixed-size summaries and escalations go up, and only what the level above can act on gets passed up.

down: intent, quotas, jobs, commands up: summaries, outcomes, escalations non-maskable: straight to a person

The chain, top to bottom

Rules that keep this shape

  1. A layer may only be as slow as the layer it waits on is patient. Reflexes never wait on the network, tactics never wait on the LLM, and no LLM is consulted inside a fight. ARCH §1.4 · C7 §4.6
  2. Down carries intent, never per-bot actions: goals, quotas, job batches. Commands name goals, operations, regions and selectors, never lists of bots. ARCH §3.1, §4.1
  3. A level escalates only what its parent can act on, after trying its own remedies, and it lists them. HQ moves bots between operations; a squad re-paths a stuck bot. ARCH §3.1
  4. Interrupts escalate on price, not on occurrence. Anything a job didn't expect is contained first and only reaches the commander when avoiding it breaks the approved quote. INTERRUPTS §2.4
  5. Settings flow down by scope (swarm, operation, squad, job, bot) and the most specific wins. The commander sets swarm and operation scope; operations and squads set the rest through job contracts and plays. CONVENTIONS §4

Where an LLM is allowed

  • The commander: goals, policies, quote approvals and escalations, through the same API a person uses. K3 §4.7
  • Operation officers, optional, for fuzzy work such as diplomacy or a raid, each seeing only its own squads. Not in v1 (AD-K5.1, decided 2026-10-15); the interface stays ready. K5 §4.10 · AD-K5.1
  • Reading people: threat assessment, and a diplomacy officer whose messages pass a person's gate. C8 §4.3, §4.7
  • The batch review of interrupts: offline, on a token budget; it proposes fixes and a person approves them. F4 §4.8

Everything from HQ down is deterministic code, and nothing on a loop faster than a few seconds waits for a model.
